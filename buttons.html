394 buttons across the shipped app, 91 of them wearing one of the 6 shared styles. This page documents all of it: the styles, the controls that go their own way, and the ones that are not buttons at all.

An audit, not a specification. It was taken ahead of the button refactor, so it records what is in the app today — 6 shared styles, 55 controls that carry their own chrome, and 394 button constructions across 155 files — rather than what a caller should reach for. Counts and call sites are swept from the source at build time. Nothing here is approved for reuse by being listed.
